How to connect

On the PM5: More Options → Turn Wireless ON. The monitor will say “Connect”. Connect from this app before starting a workout on the PM5, then start rowing — a “Just Row” or any programmed piece works.

iPhone: Safari can't use Bluetooth, so open this page in the free Bluefy browser (App Store), or install a Web Bluetooth Safari extension on iOS 26+.

Android / Mac / Windows / ChromeOS: open it in Chrome or Edge. Add it to your home screen for a full-screen app.

Demo mode: tap Try demo below to drive the app with a simulated PM5.

Data

Everything stays on your phone (IndexedDB) — nothing is uploaded. Per-stroke data includes power, drive length and time, recovery time, peak and average handle force (converted from the PM5's lbf to newtons), work per stroke, and the full force curve when the PM5 firmware provides it. CSV export gives you one row per stroke, ready for R or Python.
