Connect

On the PM5: More Options → Turn Wireless ON. Connect from here before starting a workout, then row — Just Row or any programmed piece.

iPhone: Safari has no Bluetooth for web pages; open this link in the free Bluefy browser. Android, Mac, Windows, ChromeOS: Chrome or Edge.

While rowing: tap anywhere on the display (or Display) to cycle Metrics → Power → Force → Pace → Stroke map. Units switches the big number between pace, watts and cal/hr. On a TV or wide screen everything shows at once — AirPlay your phone, or set Layout to Dashboard. Keyboard: ← → and U.

Your data

Sessions stay on this device (IndexedDB) — nothing is uploaded. Each saved session opens a full analysis in History and exports as JSON: one record per stroke with its force curve, plus the pace / rate / heart-rate time series.
